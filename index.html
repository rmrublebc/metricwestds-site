What we do

Metric West Development Solutions is the company behind Case-Flo Pro. We focus on product engineering, hosting, and the platform side of HIPAA-minded software — so agencies can run care workflows without building infrastructure from scratch.

  • Product engineering

    Clinical documentation tools, caseload workflows, and agency operations software.

  • Platform stewardship

    Hosting, vendors, and Business Associate responsibilities for the applications we operate.

  • Practical compliance support

    Audit and evidence tooling built into products — separate from each agency’s own compliance program.

Flagship product

Case-Flo Pro

Case-Flo Pro is a clinical operations application for behavioral health teams — documentation, caseload, and related workflows — with a dedicated compliance workspace for agencies.

Agencies log into Case-Flo Pro on its own domain. This Metric West site does not host clinical data or app login.
