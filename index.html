Flagship product

Case-Flo Pro

Case-Flo Pro is a clinical operations application for behavioral health teams. Clinicians manage caseloads, write notes, and work through assessments and plans (including FBA and BSP), quarterly reporting, and guardian or agency approvals. Agency administrators get team setup, client assignment, oversight dashboards, and a dedicated compliance workspace.

The platform is customizable based on agency needs — so documentation flows, roles, and approval processes can match how your organization actually delivers care.

Agencies log into Case-Flo Pro on its own domain. This Metric West site does not host clinical data or app login. Screenshots below are from the shared demo sandbox (synthetic data only).
